BounceStyles is a cosmetics mod, designed to allow anyone to create, add, and share their own custom cosmetics into Minecraft using "Style Packs".

Using Geckolib, you can create your own Style Packs, and add cosmetics that players can unlock and equip to their Head, Body, Legs, or Feet. You can also specify a number of settings to adjust how your Style appears in game, including hiding particular player body parts or armor slots when equipped.

Players can choose what Styles to equip in their respective slots in the Wardrobe UI, accessible via Keybind, and save presets of Style combinations to reuse.

Server Admins can also create itemized versions of Styles in the form of Style Magazines to allow players to unlock particular Styles, or unlock/re-lock Styles via commands.

[**More details on creating Styles can be found on the wiki**](https://github.com/BounceSMP/BounceStyles/wiki) *(A wiki rework is planned, current state is not final)*
